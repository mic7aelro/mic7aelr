# Writing

This file contains ideas for the future `/writing` section. Each article should have a clear opinion, a specific subject, and a personal reason for writing it.

## Pop culture

### The superhero Mount Rushmore

- Choose four superheroes who define the medium.
- Compare cultural impact, publishing history, adaptations, and influence.
- Explain the selection rules before naming the four heroes.
- Consider separate lists for comic books, films, and personal favorites.

## Film

### Reviews

- Review *The Odyssey*.
- Publish short reviews from Letterboxd as expanded essays.
- Review new releases without summarizing the full plot.
- Revisit films after a second viewing.

### Rankings

- Rank my favorite films.
- Rank films by a specific director, actor, franchise, or decade.
- Explain how the ranking criteria affect the result.

### Movie spotlights

- Select one film and explain why it matters.
- Focus on one element, such as direction, editing, production design, music, or performance.
- Connect the film to another work or a larger cultural idea.

## Chess

### Nakamura versus Gelfand

- Annotate a selected Hikaru Nakamura and Boris Gelfand game.
- Explain the opening plan in plain language.
- Identify the critical position and the decisive mistake.
- Show the practical choices available to both players.

### Additional formats

- Analyze one instructive game at a time.
- Review important positions without reproducing the complete game.
- Compare engine recommendations with practical human decisions.

## Dungeons & Dragons

### Sorcerer, warlock, and wizard power scale

- Compare the three classes at low, middle, and high levels.
- Separate combat power, utility, flexibility, and role-playing value.
- Account for spell lists, resources, subclasses, and party composition.
- Explain which class is strongest for each type of player.

### Additional formats

- Build guides with a clear level range and campaign goal.
- Compare subclasses under the same rules.
- Review character concepts that are effective and easy to play.

## Music

### Album reviews

- Review favorite albums.
- Explain the context, sound, themes, and personal connection.
- Select the essential tracks without rating every song.
- Revisit albums whose reputation changed over time.

### Favorite albums

- Publish a ranked list with short notes.
- Create separate lists by genre, decade, artist, or mood.
- Update the main list when a new album earns a place.

## Repeatable article formats

- **Review:** State the opinion, support it with examples, and give a clear conclusion.
- **Spotlight:** Examine one work, person, game, team, or idea.
- **Ranking:** Define the criteria before presenting the order.
- **Analysis:** Explain how something works and why the details matter.
- **Retrospective:** Revisit a subject after time has changed its context.
